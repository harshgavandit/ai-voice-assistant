# AI Voice Assistant

Speak a story idea, and the app turns it into a short adventure you can read and listen to.

It transcribes your recording, writes a story based on what you said, and reads the story aloud. The stories use simple language and focus on kid-friendly adventures.

[Try the app](https://ai-voice-assistant-steel.vercel.app/)

You can:

- Record an idea with your microphone or upload an audio file.
- Read the transcript and the generated story.
- Listen to the story and replay it using the audio controls.
- Record a follow-up or start a new conversation.
- Use the included demo without recording anything.
